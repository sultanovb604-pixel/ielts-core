Task 2 Band 8.5–9.0 Model Discussion + Opinion

Factors Behind Success in Life

IELTS Essay Prompt

Some people think that success in life comes from hard work and determination, while others think that there are other factors. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

Introduction

It is widely believed that diligence and perseverance are the primary drivers of accomplishment in life, whereas others contend that external elements, such as socioeconomic background, innate talent, and serendipity, play an equally decisive role. While I acknowledge the profound contribution of fortune and privilege, I firmly maintain that sustained effort and personal resolve remain the bedrock upon which meaningful and enduring success is built.

Body Paragraph 1 — External Factors (Opportunity & Fortune)

On the one hand, advocates of the view that success depends on factors beyond personal industry put forward compelling arguments. Chief among these is the undeniable advantage conferred by socioeconomic status. Individuals born into affluent families typically enjoy access to elite education, influential networks, and financial safety nets that mitigate the risks of failure. Furthermore, inherent cognitive or physical gifts cannot be overlooked; elite athletes or mathematical prodigies clearly possess biological predispositions that hard work alone cannot replicate. Finally, serendipity — being in the right place at the right time — frequently serves as the catalyst for extraordinary breakthroughs, such as early-stage technology entrepreneurs catching an emerging market wave.

Body Paragraph 2 — Internal Factors (Hard Work & Tenacity)

On the other hand, it is hard work and determination that convert latent potential and fleeting opportunities into tangible achievement. Even the most gifted individuals fail to excel without rigorous practice and discipline. As psychological research into deliberate practice demonstrates, mastery across virtually every domain requires thousands of hours of focused application. Moreover, determination provides the psychological resilience needed to overcome inevitable setbacks. While privilege may open initial doors, sustaining a career or building an enterprise through economic downturns and personal crises demands unwavering tenacity and grit.

Conclusion

In conclusion, although external circumstances, biological predispositions, and sheer luck undeniably shape human outcomes, they are largely insufficient on their own. It is relentless effort, discipline, and emotional resilience that ultimately determine whether individuals fulfill their potential and achieve enduring success.
