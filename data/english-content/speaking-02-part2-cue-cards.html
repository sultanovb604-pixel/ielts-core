Describe a memorable journey you went on.
  • Where you went
  • Who you went with
  • What happened during the journey
  • And explain why this journey was so unforgettable to you
Band 8.5+ Model Narrative:
"I’d like to talk about an exhilarating road trip I took two summers ago to the mountainous landscapes of the Tian Shan range near Chimgan. I embarked on this trip alongside three of my closest childhood companions, as we wanted to celebrate our university graduation in an unforgettable setting.

We set off early at the crack of dawn to beat the morning rush hour. The initial part of the drive was smooth, but as we ascended into the winding mountain passes, the scenery transformed into dramatic, craggy peaks and lush alpine valleys. Halfway through the trek, our car had a sudden minor tire puncture in an isolated area. Rather than becoming disgruntled, we treated it as an unexpected adventure, working collectively to replace the wheel while soaking in the panoramic vistas.

Ultimately, what made this trip so indelibly etched in my memory was not just the picturesque backdrop, but the sheer camaraderie and unscripted moments we shared away from the relentless digital distractions of everyday life."
