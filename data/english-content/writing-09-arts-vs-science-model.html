Task 2 Band 8.5–9.0 Model Two-Part Question

The Relevance of Arts vs Science & Technology

IELTS Essay Prompt

In an era of rapid technological progress, the arts continue to command widespread respect and appreciation. What are the reasons for this? Are the arts as important as science and technology?

Introduction

In an era defined by rapid scientific advancement and technological innovation, it might be assumed that artistic pursuits would lose their societal relevance. However, the arts continue to command widespread respect and appreciation across diverse cultures, and there are compelling reasons for their enduring stature. In my view, while science and technology drive material prosperity, the arts fulfill an equally vital, complementary role in cultivating human empathy, cultural identity, and moral awareness.

Body Paragraph 1 — Why the Arts Retain Enduring Stature

The primary reason the arts maintain enduring value is their unique capacity to address human emotion, cultural narrative, and ethical meaning — dimensions of life that empirical science cannot fully capture. While scientific disciplines excel at deciphering physical laws and solving technical problems, they offer limited guidance on questions of morality, purpose, and subjective experience. Literature, for example, serves as a powerful instrument for exploring national identity and collective memory; Abdulla Qodiriy’s seminal novel O'tkan Kunlar (Days Gone By) offers readers a profound, intimate comprehension of early 20th-century social conditions far more evocatively than a standard historical archive ever could. Similarly, visual art, architecture, and music shape our perception of beauty and provide catharsis, enriching mental and emotional well-being.

Body Paragraph 2 — Arts and Science as Complementary Pillars

Regarding whether the arts are as important as science and technology, I believe that directly pitting them against each other represents a false dichotomy, as they serve fundamentally different yet equally essential purposes. Technological breakthroughs, such as medical vaccines, agricultural mechanization, and clean energy systems, undeniably transform global living conditions and physical survival. Nevertheless, the arts give that survival meaning: they foster critical reflection, preserve intangible heritage, and facilitate intercultural dialogue. Consequently, treating science and art as mutually exclusive ignores how closely they interact; historical polymaths like Leonardo da Vinci demonstrated that scientific creativity and artistic imagination often spring from the same cognitive roots.

Conclusion

In conclusion, the arts endure because they fulfill psychological, cultural, and spiritual needs that technical innovation alone cannot satisfy. Both domains are indispensable, serving complementary functions that together sustain a balanced, enlightened, and flourishing human society.
