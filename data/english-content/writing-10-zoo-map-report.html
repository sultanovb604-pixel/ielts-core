Task 1 Band 9.0 Model Report Map & Spatial Transformation

Australian Zoo Layout (1990 vs Current)

IELTS Task 1 Prompt

The maps compare the layout of an Australian zoo in 1990 with its current arrangement. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Introduction & Paraphrase

The provided maps illustrate the spatial modifications undertaken at an Australian zoo between 1990 and the present day.

Overall Overview

Overall, the zoo has undergone comprehensive modernisation and commercial expansion. While foundational features such as the western entrance, gift shop, and elephant enclosure have remained in their original positions, the central and eastern sections have been extensively redeveloped to incorporate novel recreational attractions, improved pedestrian pathways, and a cable car transport network.

Body Paragraph 1 — Western and Southern Transformations

In 1990, visitors entering through the main western gate found a gift shop to the immediate south and an elephant enclosure directly to the north. These structures have been preserved unaltered. However, the open grass parkland to the south-east of the entrance has been repurposed: the original picnic zone has been relocated further east, making way for a modern café and an interactive discovery centre. The original southern walking trail has been upgraded into a paved promenade connecting to a new cable car station in the south-east corner.

Body Paragraph 2 — Central and Northern/Eastern Expansions

The central and northern sectors have witnessed the most dramatic changes. The central lake, previously surrounded by open paddocks for kangaroos, has been transformed into a bird show arena with amphitheatre seating. The kangaroos have been relocated to an expanded enclosure in the north-east, which also houses a new Australian nocturnal wildlife exhibit. Additionally, the historic administrative building in the north has been converted into a heritage museum, flanked by a secondary cable car terminal that ferries visitors across the facility from the southern perimeter.

Spatial Vocabulary & Transformation Lexis

undergone comprehensive modernisation Experienced complete structural updating and facility renewal.
preserved unaltered / remained intact Retained without any structural or physical alterations.
repurposed to make way for Converted from its previous function to allow space for a new facility.
relocated to an expanded enclosure Moved to a newly created, substantially larger territory.
flanked by a secondary terminal Bordered or positioned immediately alongside another building.
ferries visitors across the perimeter Transports passengers efficiently from one boundary of the site to another.
