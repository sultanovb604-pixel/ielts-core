IELTS Speaking · Part 1

Core Interview Topics & High-Band Model Answers

Master fluent, natural 3-sentence answers for the most frequent introductory interview topics with examiner vocabulary.

Topic 1: Hometown & Living Area
Q1: Where is your hometown located?
"I was born and raised in Samarkand, which is an ancient, historic city located in southeastern Uzbekistan. It's world-renowned for its breathtaking Islamic architecture and vibrant cultural heritage."
Band 8+ Lexis: born and raised, historic city, world-renowned, vibrant cultural heritage
Q2: What do you like most about living there?
"Without a doubt, it's the warm hospitality of the locals and the peaceful ambience. Even though it attracts a steady stream of tourists year-round, our residential neighbourhoods remain surprisingly serene and tightly-knit."
Band 8+ Lexis: without a doubt, warm hospitality, steady stream, tightly-knit
Topic 2: Work & Studies
Q1: Do you work or are you a student?
"Currently, I'm a final-year undergraduate majoring in Information Systems at university. Alongside my academic coursework, I also do freelance web development to gain hands-on commercial experience."
Band 8+ Lexis: final-year undergraduate, majoring in, academic coursework, hands-on commercial experience
Q2: Why did you choose this field?
"I've always had a profound fascination with modern technology and algorithmic problem-solving. It's a rapidly evolving domain that offers boundless career prospects and continuous intellectual growth."
Band 8+ Lexis: profound fascination, rapidly evolving domain, boundless career prospects
Topic 3: Daily Routine & Time Management
Q1: What is the busiest part of your day?
"Typically, the morning hours between 9 AM and midday are the most demanding. That's when I tackle cognitively intensive tasks like analytical study and project deadlines, before meetings commence."
Band 8+ Lexis: typically, demanding, tackle cognitively intensive tasks, commence
