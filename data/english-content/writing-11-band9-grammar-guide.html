IELTS Academic & General Grammar Mastery 9.0 Grammatical Range & Accuracy

Band 9.0 Grammar Structures

Master these 5 advanced syntactic patterns to demonstrate sophisticated Grammatical Range and Accuracy (GRA) across Task 1 and Task 2 essays.

1. Inverted Conditionals

Structure 1
Should + Subject + Verb... (Type 1)
Were + Subject + to-Infinitive... (Type 2)
Had + Subject + Past Participle... (Type 3)

Omit the conjunction if and invert the auxiliary verb to signal formal academic style immediately.

Band 9.0 Examples:

• Type 1: Should governments fail to regulate carbon emissions, catastrophic ecological shifts will occur.

• Type 2: Were policymakers to invest heavily in public transport, urban gridlock would subside rapidly.

• Type 3: Had urban planners anticipated this demographic surge, current housing shortages could have been averted.

Standard (Band 6.0–6.5) If governments don't act soon, pollution will get much worse.
Inverted Band 9.0 Should state authorities fail to intervene promptly, environmental degradation will intensify significantly.

2. Academic Nominalisation

Structure 2
Verb / Adjective ➔ Abstract Noun Phrase (e.g. degrade ➔ degradation)

Convert verbs and adjectives into dense noun phrases. This creates the authoritative, objective tone characteristic of academic prose.

Band 9.0 Example:

The unrelenting expansion of urban centers has precipitated the acute deterioration of air quality and infrastructural resilience.

Verb-Heavy (Band 6.0) Because cities are growing very quickly, air is becoming dirtier and traffic is getting worse.
Nominalised Band 9.0 Rapid urban expansion has catalyzed severe air pollution and chronic traffic congestion.

3. Cleft Sentences for Focus

Structure 3
It is + [Target Element] + that / who...
What + [Clause] + is / was + [Focus]...

Split a single clause into two to place intense rhetorical emphasis on your key claim or policy solution.

Band 9.0 Examples:

• It-Cleft: It is early childhood education, rather than punitive legislation, that exerts the most enduring influence on social mobility.

• Wh-Cleft: What modern healthcare systems urgently require is an aggressive reorientation toward preventative medicine.

4. Concessive Clauses with Fronting

Structure 4
Adjective / Adverb + as / though + Subject + Verb, Main Clause...

Front an adjective with as or though to concede a counterargument elegantly before dismantling it.

Band 9.0 Examples:

• Compelling as the economic argument for fossil fuels may appear, it completely ignores the irreversible cost of environmental degradation.

• Disruptive though automation may be in the short term, its long-term productivity yields are indisputable.

5. Participial Phrases (-ing & -ed)

Structure 5
[Present Participle / Past Participle Phrase], Main Subject + Main Verb...

Eliminate unnecessary relative clauses and conjunctions by beginning sentences with concise participial modifiers.

Band 9.0 Examples:

• Past Participle (-ed): Driven by escalating competition in the global labor market, university graduates increasingly seek postgraduate credentials.

• Present Participle (-ing): Recognizing the catastrophic risks of uncurbed emissions, municipal leaders are establishing zero-emission zones across major capitals.
