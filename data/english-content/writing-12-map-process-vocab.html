Task 1 Practice Map & Process Lexical Accuracy

Map & Process Vocabulary & Error Correction

Section A: Spatial Prepositions & Map Collocations

  • 1. The hospital is situated on the southern outskirts, adjacent with adjacent to the industrial zone. Rule: The adjective adjacent strictly takes the preposition to, never with.
  • 2. A bypass links the suburbs to the centre, which it replaces replacing / which replaces the old ring road. Rule: Avoid pronoun redundancy (*which it*); use a non-defining relative clause or a present participle.
  • 3. The town centre underwent significant redevelopment, with Victorian buildings demolished for modern retail units to make way for modern retail units. Rule: The formal academic collocation is demolished to make way for.
  • 4. The residential sector was extended in the east direction eastward / to the east. Rule: Use directional adverbs (*eastward*, *north-westward*) or prepositional phrases (*to the east*).

Section B: Process Verbs & Cyclical Sequencing

  • 1. Cultivation & Harvesting: Bamboo stalks are harvested in the autumn after a four-year maturation period.
  • 2. Mechanical Treatment: The raw stalks are shredded and crushed into fibrous strips before being soaked in chemical solvents.
  • 3. Filtration & Extrusion: The softened pulp is filtered, separating long cellulose fibres, and extruded through spinnerets to produce fine yarn.
  • 4. Weaving & Manufacture: In the culminating phase, the spun yarn is woven into breathable textile sheets and tailored into commercial garments.
