IELTS Writing Task 2

Advanced introduction bank.

Human-written opening paragraphs, edited for grammar, punctuation and clarity. Use them to study position, paraphrasing and thesis structure.

Study tip: analyse the structure, then write your own version. Memorising an entire introduction is less useful than learning how each sentence works.

Opinion essay

Roads or public transport

As urban populations continue to expand, policymakers face mounting pressure to improve transport infrastructure, particularly through the construction of new highways. While some contend that expanding road networks is essential, I firmly believe that prioritising public transport is a more sustainable and effective response to modern transport challenges.

Agree or disagree

Luck and success

Many people believe that success in life is mainly the result of luck rather than hard work. I completely disagree with this view because consistent effort, sound decisions and determination play a far greater role in achieving long-term success.

Discussion essay

University and career success

Some people consider university education unnecessary for career success, while others see a degree as essential for securing a well-paid profession. In my view, career outcomes depend largely on the knowledge and practical skills a person develops, whether these are acquired at university or through another credible route.

Discussion essay

Online and conventional classrooms

Some people predict that online education will eventually replace conventional classrooms, whereas others believe face-to-face teaching will remain indispensable. Both perspectives have merit, and the most effective model is likely to combine the flexibility of digital learning with the guidance and social interaction of a physical classroom.

Discuss both views

Career or personal life

It is often argued that young people should concentrate on building their careers, while others believe that personal life deserves greater attention. This essay will examine both views before explaining why a sustainable balance between professional ambition and personal wellbeing is preferable.

Discuss both views

Economic growth or the environment

Some people believe governments should prioritise economic growth, whereas others maintain that environmental protection should come first. This essay will consider both positions and argue that lasting prosperity depends on policies that advance the economy without damaging the natural systems on which it relies.

Advantages and disadvantages

Living alone

In many countries, a growing number of people are choosing to live alone rather than with family members. Although this trend can provide greater independence and privacy, it may also increase loneliness, stress and financial pressure.

Advantages and disadvantages

Remote work

Many organisations now allow employees to work remotely. While working from home may reduce commuting time and give staff greater flexibility, it can also create isolation and blur the boundary between professional and personal life; overall, however, I believe its advantages are greater.

Problem and solution

Urban traffic

Traffic congestion has become a major problem in many metropolitan areas, partly because their populations and dependence on private cars have grown rapidly. If left unaddressed, this issue can damage public health, daily life and economic productivity, making coordinated transport and planning reforms essential.

Cause and effect

Teen stress and digital devices

High levels of stress and depression are increasingly reported among adolescents. Although these problems have several causes, excessive and poorly managed use of social media and digital devices can intensify social comparison, disrupt sleep and weaken young people's mental health.

Cause and effect

Air pollution

Air pollution has become a serious concern in many countries, driven largely by industrial emissions, vehicle exhaust and widespread dependence on fossil fuels. Without decisive action, it will continue to harm public health, ecosystems and the overall quality of urban life.

Two-part question

Spending on experiences

Some people prefer to spend money on experiences, such as travel or live events, rather than on physical possessions. This choice is often motivated by the lasting memories, personal growth and social connection that meaningful experiences can provide.
