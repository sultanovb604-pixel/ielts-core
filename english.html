Can I start for free?

Yes. Create a free account to explore starter materials and the practice workspace. Premium gives you access to additional resources. Compare plans.

What can I practise here?

Practise Academic Reading and Listening with tests and answer review. You can also explore Writing examples, Speaking prompts, English foundations, and vocabulary resources in the library.

Are the band scores official IELTS results?

No. Scores are practice estimates to help guide your preparation. IELTS Core is an independent learning platform, not an official IELTS test provider.

Can I practise on my phone?

Yes. The practice workspace adapts to smaller screens. For the closest experience to a computer-delivered test, we recommend a laptop or desktop and headphones for Listening.
