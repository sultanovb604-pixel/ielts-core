Before you choose

Can I try the platform for free?

Yes. Create a free account to explore starter materials. Premium opens additional practice resources.

Will my plan renew automatically?

No. Each plan is a one-time payment for the selected duration.

How is Premium activated?

Contact @ieltscoreadmin, confirm payment details, and send your receipt to support. Activation follows payment verification; support will confirm when access is ready.

Are these official IELTS results?

No. IELTS Core is an independent preparation platform. Practice band scores are estimates, not official test results.
