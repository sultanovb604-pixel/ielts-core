Library Services: Good morning. Library services.
Caller: Oh, good morning. My family and I have recently moved to the area, and we’d like to find where our nearest library is. We don’t have a car.
Library Services: Right. So whereabouts are you? In Perthon, in the West.
Caller: Fine.
Library Services: So I’ll just get the details on screen for you. Okay. Your nearest one is the Minack Library.
Caller: Minack?
Library Services: Yes. In Skellarn Street. [Q1 → Skellarn]
Caller: Can you spell that?
Library Services: It’s S K E L L A R N Street. It’s very easy to find because it’s beside a park. [Q2 → park]
Caller: Oh, I think I know that area. There’s a sports center near there, isn’t there?
Library Services: I don’t know of one.
Caller: Oh, I’m probably mistaken. Anyway, it’s probably a bit far for us to walk from home.
Library Services: Are there any buses? Yes. It’s well served by public transport. There’s a stop about five minutes walk away.
Caller: Oh, right. And when does the library open? We’ll most often want to come at the weekend.
Library Services: I’m afraid it’s not open on Sunday.
Caller: Saturday?
Library Services: Opens at 09:00, closes at 04:30. We shut at five on weekdays. [Q3 → 4.30]
Caller: That’s fine. Now I’ve got two kids, 14 and nine, and I wondered whether you have any special activities for them. In our last place, we had an art club.
Library Services: Well, the library does run a drama club, but that would only be suitable for your younger child. It’s open for five to 13 year olds. [Q4 → drama]
Caller: But pity it’s not open to both of them. Have you anything they could both go to?
Library Services: Well, there’s no reading club that would suit them both. Oh, but there’s a singing club that’s held every second Saturday afternoon for children of all ages up to 16. [Q5 → singing]
Caller: Excellent.

Caller: Now because we’re new to the area, I’m also wanting to get involved in local activities.
Library Services: Well, Minack does some sessions for adults as well. There’s a book of the month club.
Caller: That’s good.
Library Services: And I know that about once every twelve months, they have a session where you can meet artists, you know, from the local area. [Q6 → artists]
Caller: How interesting. Well, that sounds like a full program. Now while I’m on the phone, can I check what the facilities are? Can you take out CDs?
Library Services: Not CDs, I’m afraid, but films, yes. [Q7 → films]
Caller: Right. I’ll make a note of that.
Library Services: Anything else you need to know?

Library Services: We have 10 computers, and we also stock several magazines, including business, health care and hobbies. [Q8 → magazines]

Library Services: And we do have a small shop on the premises where we sell postcards. We don’t have many, but it’s a popular service.
